Title: Rounding Off To End Price In Certain Number

Rounding off to a specific end price is a pricing strategy that helps to create more appealing prices for customers. It can make the prices look cleaner or more attractive. The Bolt Bulk Editor app can be used to round off the prices for both, the decimal digits as well as the integral digits before the decimal point.
Examples:
Say the current price of a product is&nbsp;$45.67. Now, you want to increase the price by 5% and end the price with a specific number.
1. End with .00 cents
The current price will increase by 5%, so $45.67 + 5% = $47.95
Rounding off cents to .00 we have 2 options: $47.00 OR $48.00
Since $48 is closer to $47.95, the app will update the price to $48.00
2. End with 0.00&nbsp;
The current price will increase by 5%, so $45.67 + 5% = $47.95
Rounding off cents to 0.00 we have 2 options: $40.00 OR $50.00
Since $50 is closer to $47.95, the app will update the price to $50.00
3.&nbsp;End with 00.90
Say the current price of a product is&nbsp;$5645.88. Now, you want to increase the price by 5% and end the price with a specific number.
The current price will increase by 5%, so $5645.88 + 5% = $5928.17
Rounding off cents to 00.90 we have 2 options: $5900.90 OR $6000.90
Since $5900 is closer to $5928.17, the app will update the price to $5900.90
4. End with 00.00
The current price will increase by 5%, so $5645.88 + 5% = $5928.17
Rounding off cents to 00.90 we have 2 options: $5900 OR $6000
Since $5900 is closer to $5928.17, the app will update the price to $5900.00

Changes to the Fair Usage Policy

Title: How Does The Flo Sku Sync Works 

The Flo SKU Sync App is simple to use and designed to help you keep your inventory accurate, organized, and up-to-date across the same shared SKUs.
After installing, the app will fetch all the products found in your store for the first time. This process might take from a few seconds to minutes, depending on the products you have in your store. Once the fetch is completed, you can turn 'ON' the Duplicate SKU Sync option from the Home Page of the app dashboard.
What Triggers a Sync?
Once enabled, the Flo SKU Sync app will automatically sync stock levels of all duplicate SKUs under the following conditions:

When an order is placed on Shopify
When a refund is processed
When the item is restocked via the app dashboard
When the stock is updated from the Shopify admin or any other app.
Notes:

The app does not yet support the syncing of Inventory States&nbsp;(On Hand, Committed, etc.) It only supports syncing the 'Available' inventory state.

Title: Multi Location Sync

The app syncs stock distributed across multiple locations.

LocationALocationBLocationCTotalProductA1030040

The Flo SKU Sync app will then force update the quantities of Product B and Product C to match Product A for each location. The inventory distribution will look as below:
LocationALocationBLocationCTotalProductB1030040ProductC1030040

The app assumes that the physical items are the same, even if the product listings are duplicated. Hence, at all times, the stock levels should be the same across all locations.

The app only syncs the&nbsp;quantity&nbsp;of products with duplicate SKUs. It does not sync or alter other product data attributes like titles, prices, or descriptions.

No, the app does not offer an undo feature for syncing. If something goes wrong, you will need to manually adjust the quantity either within the app or directly from your Shopify store.

Title: Dynamic Product Metafields

The Clean app allows creating tables that display the value of any product metafield created by other apps.&nbsp;
A.&nbsp;The product metafields can be displayed by adding the {{ cleantm.namespace.key&nbsp;}} dynamic data field in the input text. Here namespace.key&nbsp;needs to be replaced with the actual meta field namespace and meta field key names.
For example&nbsp;- If you have a metafield with the namespace 'instructions' and key 'wash' then to display the value of this metafield, add text {{ cleantm.instructions.wash&nbsp;}}. &nbsp;
You can combine Product Metafield Display Conditions with Dynamic Metafields to create powerful rules that display metafield data based on your rules.
Below are a few examples of product metafield representations that may be helpful.
Metafield TypeProduct Metafield RepresentationProduct{{ cleantm.custom.product.value }}Collection&nbsp;{{ cleantm.custom.collection.value }}Money{{ cleantm.custom.price.value | money }}

Title: Dynamic Variant Metafields

Title: Button Visibility How It Works

This guide explains how the “View on Amazon” button appears to customers based on the region settings you configure in the Linkr app.
1. Region-Based Button Display (Show View on Amazon Button based on Customer’s Region)When you activate a product for a specific Amazon region, the button will be shown only to customers visiting from that region.

How to enable this:Activate the product for the desired Amazon region in the Linkr app.
Add the Linkr app block to your theme.
Under Force Marketplace, select Default&nbsp;and Click Save.
With this setup, customers will see the “View on Amazon” button only if they are located in an activated region.

Example:If you activate the product for Amazon US, visitors from the United States will see → View on Amazon (US)&nbsp;
If you activate the same product for multiple regions (US, UK, CA, AU), the app will automatically display the correct button based on the customer’s IP location:

Visitor from Canada → View on Amazon (CA)
Visitor from United Kingdom → View on Amazon (UK)
2. Show One Amazon Region’s Button in Additional CountriesYou can activate a product for one Amazon region and choose additional countries where you want the same button to appear.
How to enable this:Activate the product for your desired Amazon region, then add the additional countries under “Show in Countries” for that region in the settings.

ExampleIf you activate the product for the United States region but also want customers from Canada or Australia to see the same “View on Amazon (US)” button, you can add those countries in the settings.
Visitors from the countries you select will also see the “View on Amazon (US)” button:

Visitor from the US → View on Amazon (US)
Visitor from Canada → View on Amazon (US)
This feature is helpful when you want a single Amazon region to serve customers from multiple countries.
3. Fixed Marketplace Button (Show the Same Button to All Visitors Worldwide)The Fixed Marketplace option allows you to display the same Amazon region button to every customer, regardless of their location. This is useful when you want all visitors to be redirected to a single Amazon marketplace.
How to enable this:

Activate the product for the desired Amazon region, then in the Linkr app block, select the appropriate marketplace under Force Marketplace and click Save.

Once enabled, all visitors worldwide will see the same Amazon marketplace button on products activated with the Linkr app.

ExampleIf you activate the product for the Canada region and select amazon.ca under Force Marketplace in the Linkr app block:

Visitors from Canada, UK, US, Australia, India, or any other country will all see → View on Amazon (CA)
This ensures all customers are redirected to the same Amazon marketplace. You can also add multiple Fixed Marketplace buttons if you want to display more than one marketplace option to all visitors.

Title: Understanding Asin Custom Link And Keyword Options

Linkr&nbsp;allows you to connect your Shopify products to Amazon listings using either an ASIN or a Custom Amazon Link.
1. ASINEnter the Amazon Standard Identification Number (ASIN), and Linkr will automatically generate the correct Amazon product link.
Example:If your product’s ASIN is B00NOY3JKW, Linkr app will generate the following Amazon link:

https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00NOY3JKW/s/?k=&amp;tag=&amp;th=1&amp;psc=1
When shoppers click the View on Amazon button, they’ll be redirected to this link.
2. Custom LinkAdd the custom Amazon product URL for maximum accuracy and control.
https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B00NOY3JKW/?tag=mystore-20&nbsp;
This option is ideal if you’re using affiliate links or region-specific Amazon URLs.

Title: Amazon Region Settings

The Linkr app displays the View on Amazon button based on the marketplace selected in the dashboard.
To ensure the button appears for customers in additional regions, you can add those regions in the Amazon Region Settings section.
Steps to Configure Region Visibility
Step 1: Open the Linkr app dashboard and click on Settings. The Amazon Region Settings page will open, displaying all supported Amazon regions.
Step 2: Click on the Edit button for the region you want to update.
Step 4: In the Edit Region screen, you will see options to:

Select Button Color&nbsp;
Add Affiliate Tag
Configure Show in Countries
Step 5: Add your affiliate tag (for example: store-20) and click Save.
Step 6: Under the Show in Countries section, click the dropdown button.
Step 7: You will see a list of regions. Select the regions where you want the View on Amazon button to be displayed.

Example: select Canada, then click Save.
Step 7: After saving, you will see the updated settings. For example, for the United States, the button will now be set to display in the US and Canada regions, along with the affiliate tag you added.&nbsp;
Similarly, you can configure region visibility for any other regions where you want the View on Amazon button to be displayed.
