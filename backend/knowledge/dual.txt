Title: How Does Dual Price App Work 

Before getting started, configure the app by changing the currencies option on your store for HTML with currency and HTML without currency option in the Shopify settings as shown in the image below:
The text to copy is available below:
&lt;div class="dualprice"&gt;${{amount}}&lt;/div&gt;
Steps to display both prices:
1. Under the settings section, firstly you need to activate the Dual app embed from your Shopify admin customize theme settings.&nbsp;
Refer to the image below to enable app embed via Shopify admin:
2. Under the settings, you may then configure whether the product's Base price is inclusive of tax or exclusive, tax rate, and other options as per your requirement.
3. Next, under the design section, input the text that you want to show adjacent to the price and set its color and font size to display the same on the product page, collection page, and home page as per your requirements.&nbsp;
4. Finally, activate the switch to display both prices and hit the 'Save' button.&nbsp;

Title: Setting Up Tax Display For Different Regions

The Market Tax Display feature in the Dual Price App allows you to configure a common tax rate for specific regions (markets). This ensures that product prices can be displayed both inclusive and exclusive of tax for selected countries, improving pricing transparency for your customers.

&nbsp;

Key Benefits

Display tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive prices for specific markets.
Maintain accurate, region-specific pricing without manual updates.
Provide clear tax breakdowns to customers, enhancing trust and clarity.

&nbsp;

How It Works ?
&nbsp;

Step 1: Enable the Dual Price App Embed
&nbsp;

From your Shopify admin, open the Customize Theme settings and enable the Dual Price App embed.
Step 2: Configure Markets
&nbsp;

Go to the Dual Price App Dashboard → Market section.
Step 3: Set Tax Rate &amp; Countries

Enter the tax rate you wish to apply.
Select the countries/markets where you want the dual price to be displayed from the dropdown list.
&nbsp;

Note: The primary market of your store is automatically excluded from the country selection list. For this market, the Dual Price App’s default settings will be applied.

&nbsp;

Step 4: Add Custom Price Class
&nbsp;

Add the Custom Class for the product price element based on your currently published theme.

Example: .price
Step 5: Save

&nbsp;

Click Save to apply your settings. The dual price display will now appear for the selected countries.
Note: Dual app market feature is available on Basic plan.
&nbsp;

If you have problems with the installation or finding difficulty, please raise a support ticket here&nbsp;or email us at support@thaliaapps.freshdesk.com and we will take care of it.

Title: Toggle Switch Customer Price Preference

This feature empowers your customers to choose how they want to view product prices—either Tax Inclusive or Tax Exclusive—directly from your store’s header section. By giving end users this control, you provide a more personalised and transparent shopping experience.

Key Benefits

Customer Choice – Let shoppers select between tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive prices.
Transparency – Improve trust by showing exactly how taxes affect final prices.
User-Friendly – The toggle switch is displayed prominently in the header for easy access.
No Manual Effort – Once configured, the app automatically updates price display based on customer preference.
How does it Works ?&nbsp;
Step 1: Configure the Toggle Switch

Navigate to the Dual Price App Dashboard → Option Settings.
Select the “Customer Preference” option to enable the toggle switch.
Step 2: Add Custom Class for Header Section
Add the Custom Class of your header element based on the theme you have published in the following format:
Syntax :&nbsp;.class-name &gt; *:first-child
Example: .search-action &gt; *:first-child
Step 3: Apply stylings (Optional)
Simply go to the design section and you can change the color of text, background, etc. by adding custom CSS in the design section of the app dashboard.&nbsp;
Step 4: Save
Click Save to apply your settings.&nbsp;
The Dual Toggle Switch will now appear in the header section of your store, allowing customers to switch between tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive views.

Title: Removing The App

Before removing the app, revert the currencies option on your store for HTML with currency and HTML without currency option in the Shopify settings as shown in the image below:
You can then uninstall the Dual Price app through your Shopify store admin.

Go to your Shopify admin.
Go to Apps'. Click on the Apps and sales channel settings.
A pop-up window will open wherein you will find the list of all the apps. Click on the 'remove' button and then delete.
That's it! The app is now uninstalled. There is no residual code or leftover files that need to be manually removed.
For detailed instructions check out the Shopify guide to uninstall the app.
Note: Once the app is uninstalled, the app subscription terminates automatically.
