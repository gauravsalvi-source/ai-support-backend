Title: How Does It Work 

1. Install the app and link your Amazon Seller account.

Install the app from the Shopify App Store. &nbsp;Then connect the app to your Amazon Seller account - follow the easy steps provided in the app dashboard.
2. Pull products from Amazon to Connectr

Click the 'Pull All' button on the app dashboard. The app will read products from your Amazon Seller account, extract relevant information, augment it with external data and store it in the Connectr database. The entire process may take from a few minutes to a few hours depending on the number of products you have. You also have to option to pull specific items from your Amazon catalog.
3. Push products from Connectr to Shopify

Once the products are pulled from the Amazon Seller account they will be visible inside the Connectr dashboard. Later you can 'Push All' or push selective products to Shopify. Pushing products to Shopify will create new products in Shopify complete with title, description, images, prices, variations, etc.&nbsp;
4. Sync products

Once the products are pushed to Shopify, the Connectr app can be used to keep the product prices and inventory quantities in sync with Amazon. There are 3 ways to keep the products in sync - more info here.
5. Fulfill Orders for Amazon products

You can choose to ship the products manually or automate the process via FBA. More info here.

Title: Updating Inventory Quantity

Connectr can be used to keep quantity in sync between Amazon and Shopify. Inventory can be updated with either of the 2 options:
Option 1: As available on Amazon&nbsp;

Connectr will update the inventory quantities as per values from Amazon.
Option 2: Fixed count&nbsp;

Connectr will set the inventory quantity on Shopify to 1, regardless of your quantity at Amazon. When you sell the product on Shopify, instead of delisting it, the quantity will set to 0 so that you don't lose your SEO on the listing. &nbsp;Later when you sync the products, the app will reset the quantity back to 1 only if that SKU is in stock on Amazon. &nbsp;This way, Connectr keeps your quantity in sync and ensures you never double-sell an item. Also, you can change the 'Fixed Count Quantity' field to set more than 1. This will update products with a quantity of this value. If you change this setting, all your existing products will be updated automatically when the app syncs.

Title: Ship With Fba

You can use the Connectr app to fulfill orders on Shopify using your Amazon FBA account. This&nbsp;is useful for the sake of&nbsp;inventory efficiency. You can leave your stock with Amazon, all in one place, and fulfill both Amazon and Shopify orders with one dashboard. Additionally, you can track both Amazon and Shopify orders in Seller Central, and your inventory will be neatly stored with one provider i.e. Amazon FBA.
HOW IT WORKS
1] Manual Mode

Go to Connectr app &gt; FBA
The FBA Orders page will list orders from Shopify.&nbsp;
Select the items you want to ship via FBA and click on the 'Ship with FBA' button.
The app will prepare an FBA shipment depending on your FBA settings in the Connectr app.
Confirm the shipment by clicking 'Ship with FBA Now'.
Your order will be sent to and fulfilled by Amazon FBA.&nbsp;
The order status will be available on the app dashboard.
Connectr app will update the Shopify order status along with the tracking information.
2] Auto Mode

Instead of manually confirming each FBA shipment you can configure the app to automatically ship with FBA for all eligible orders on Shopify.

Go to Connectr app &gt; FBA &gt; Settings
Select AUTO Mode and hit Save.
Each new order on Shopify will then be attempted to be shipped via FBA.&nbsp;
You will receive confirmation of success or failure via email.
The order status will be available on the app dashboard.
Connectr will update the Shopify order status along with the tracking information.
Note:

Ship with FBA is priced at $6/month.
It is available in the following regions only: US, Canada, Mexico, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Australia. Other regions coming soon.

Title: Shipping Multi Item Orders In Auto Mode

Ship with FBA is designed to work with multi-item orders on Shopify. Each line item in the order is attempted to be fulfilled via FBA. In case one or more SKUs is not fulfillable (due invalid SKU, insufficient inventory, etc) then you can configure the app to either of the following options:
1. Fulfill partial order

If this option is selected, the app will partially fulfill the order by shipping the items having valid SKUs. Invalid SKUs will be skipped.
2. Do not fulfill the order

In this case, the order will not be shipped at all, if it contains one or more invalid items. Only if all the line item SKUs of the order are valid, then the order will be shipped via FBA.

Title: Ship With Fba Fulfilling Orders But Not Updating Shopify Troubleshooting 

If Connectr app successfully ships via FBA but does not update the status of orders on Shopify or does not update the tracking information, then it could be due to the following reasons:
1. Misconfigured Shopify Order Processing settings:
Go to Shopify admin &gt; Settings &gt; General &gt; 'Order processing' section
Set 'After an order has been paid' to 'Do not automatically fulfill any of the order's line items' option.
If 'After an order has been paid' is set to 'Automatically fulfill the order‘s line items' then the Connectr app will not update Shopify order status.
2.&nbsp;Shopify's built-in 'Fulfillment by Amazon' integration is active:
If you use the Connectr app to ship products via FBA then you need to deactivate Shopify's built-in 'Fulfillment by Amazon' integration. This integration prevents the Connectr app to update the Shopify order status correctly. Secondly, keeping it activated might lead to multiple shippings for the same order.
Steps to deactivate 'Fulfillment by Amazon' are provided here.

Title: Guide To Avoid Getting Your Amazon Associate Account Banned

Amazon has ramped up its compliance game significantly in the last few months. Let us walk you through the top reasons we’ve seen and heard that gets Amazon affiliates in trouble and sometimes even removed from the program.&nbsp;
Links In Email

Amazon explicitly bans any use of affiliate links in “offline promotion or in any other offline manner,” and it can surprise some new users that that applies to email too. While there are a number of notable influencers that include Amazon links in their regular email newsletters this is forbidden in section four, title “Promotional Limitations” of the Policies page for Amazon’s Operating Agreement. Instead, you can promote links in the email that point to your Shopify store having Amazon affiliate products.
Links In eBooks or PDF documents

If someone just finished your book they probably enjoyed it and it’s probably a really good time to encourage them to buy another of your books, especially if it’s part of a series. Unfortunately, another great way to get kicked out of the Amazon Associates program is to include affiliate links in your book.
Mentioning Prices (or availability)

It seems natural to add the prices on the product pages of your site while you are promoting Amazon products. The bummer is that unless you are dynamically updating that price then you are in violation of the Operating Agreement. For good reason, Amazon doesn’t want you making inaccurate or out of date claims about their products. They require that information is never more than 24 hours old. Amazon mentions this in section two of the Policies page. There is a simple way to avoid this problem: Activate auto-sync&nbsp;to enable the Connectr app to update the prices and availability of all the Amazon products once every 24 hours.
Not Including An Affiliate Disclaimer

You may make the mental argument that an affiliate disclaimer might scare people away or make your site look cluttered but it’s against the law and Amazon’s Operating Agreement! Amazon is a big fan of transparency and it’s important that their affiliates are on the right side of the law!

Your site needs to have a disclaimer explaining that you are an Affiliate, and will earn a small commission from sold products. More info here.
Affiliate Link Shortening or Link Cloaking / Hiding

Amazon is very concerned with affiliates misdirecting the audience, as the reputation of the Amazon depends on how affiliates are referring the site to visitors. The e-commerce giant wants users to know where they are being directed to. For this reason, Amazon bans link shortening that doesn’t make it obvious that you are sending users to Amazon. Connectr app does not shorten, cloak or hide affiliate links to ensure 100% compliance.
Using Your Affiliate Link for Items You Purchase

It’d be a pretty sweet deal if you could do all of your shopping online via Amazon using your affiliate link, and earn some Affiliates commission while you do. Unfortunately for thrifty shoppers, Amazon has expressly forbidden this practice. Rule #29 of the Participation Requirements states:

You will not purchase any Product(s) through Special Links for use by you or for resale or commercial use of any kind.&nbsp;
Link from Sexually Explicit and/or Violent Sites

Amazon clearly forbids using affiliate links on site that contain or promote sexually explicit materials.
Violate Amazon’s Trademark Policy

Amazon can shut down things quickly if they believe you are in violation of their trademark rights. To clarify, Amazon owns a ton of trademarks, not all of which have anything to do with the phrase “Amazon.” Proprietary products like Kindle are trademarked too, and you can quickly violate these trademarks by including trademark information in your site’s Domain name. However, you can use a trademarked name in your product link, title or description.

OK – Abc.com/amazon-kindle-ebook-reader-review

Not OK – KindleReviews.com
No Sales In 90 Days

Once you sign up for the Amazon Affiliate program, you need to sell at least 1 product within 90 days, else you will no longer be an Amazon affiliate.

However, the good news is, you can re-apply to become Amazon affiliate anytime later only on these circumstances.
