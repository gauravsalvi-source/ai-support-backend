Title: Who Is This App For 

The Sleek GST Invoices – India is for Indian Shopify merchants who need to create GST-compliant invoices. It automates the calculation of CGST, SGST, and IGST based on customer location, ensuring adherence to Indian tax regulations. It also provides/supports professional invoice templates with your logo.

Title: Email Verification

To ensure that your invoice emails are delivered successfully and are not flagged as spam or spoofed, you need to verify your email address. Follow these steps to complete the verification:
Go to your Sleek dashboard &gt; Navigate to Settings &gt; Email &gt;&nbsp;Enter your desired "From" email address in the input box &gt; Click Verify Email.
An email (from no-reply-sleek@thaliatechnologies.com)&nbsp;will be sent to the address you provided. Open this email and click on the verification link. The email that you will receive will look like this:
After clicking the link, you will be redirected back to your dashboard where you will see a "Verified" message.
&nbsp;
Note:

If you don’t see the verification email in your inbox, check your spam or junk folder.
To update your email address, follow the same steps with the new address.
Verifying your email address will help ensure that invoice emails are delivered properly and avoid being marked as spam.

Title: Send Invoices Automatically Manually

With the Sleek App dashboard, you can send invoices to the customer’s email address either automatically or manually. &nbsp;For this, you need to verify the "from" email address and configure the other details in the dashboard. Please find the guide link here&nbsp;on how you can verify email address and why is it needed.
Once done, to send Invoices Automatically,&nbsp; you need to select as to when would you like to auto-send an email having the GST compliant invoice to the customer. &nbsp;The options are:

When order is created
When order is Paid
When order is Fulfilled
Additionally, if you have a internal team that you would like to notify as well along with the customer, you can send an email to them as well. You simply need to add them in the CC or BCC section and configure the Send email to "All".&nbsp;
OR you can set to only 'CC' and 'BCC' to only send it to the internal team.
2. To Send Invoices Manually -
For each invoice, Under Action Column &gt; Click on Select &gt; From Dropdown &gt; choose Email Invoice Option &gt;A pop-up modal will open and check the details and click on send button.
The email icon turns green when an invoice is successfully sent to the customer.
