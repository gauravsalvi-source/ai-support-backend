Title: Who Is This App For 

Shipr is designed for Shopify merchants who use Amazon FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) to store and ship their inventory. This app helps automate order fulfillment by syncing Shopify orders with Amazon FBA, streamlining the shipping process.
Key Benefits:• Automated Fulfillment: Orders placed on Shopify are sent to Amazon FBA for fulfillment.
• Real-Time Sync: Keep inventory and tracking information up-to-date between platforms.
• Time &amp; Cost Savings:&nbsp;Reduce manual work and streamline order processing.

-----------------------------------

Title: How It Works

Here is a step by step guide to illustrate how the Shipr app works.
Step 1 - Install Amazon FBA Shipr App. Connect your Amazon Seller account, configure your preferences, and activate 'Ship with FBA' via the app dashboard.
Step 2 - A customer places an order on Shopify. Shipr app reads the order and it can be seen in the app dashboard.
Step 3 - Based on the order data, the Shipr app initiates a fulfillment request on Amazon FBA. This step can be manual or automated based on your settings.
Step 4 - As the shipment gets confirmed by Amazon FBA, the Shipr app updates the order status as 'Fulfilled' on Shopify. The customers receive a confirmation email.
Step 5 - After a few hours, Amazon FBA makes the tracking information available. The Shipr app updates the Shopify order with the same tracking information. The customer receives a tracking notification as per the settings in Shopify.
Step 6 - Changes in shipment status are visible in the app dashboard.
Step 7 - Amazon delivers the package to the customer. Shipr app updates the Shopify order as 'Delivered' and the customer receives the notification.

Title: Fulfilling Shopify Orders

This guide is for Shopify merchants also selling the same products on Amazon.
Fulfilling Shopify orders for FBM products

Fulfilled By Merchant, popularly known as FBM, is when sellers list their products on Amazon and choose to ship products to each buyer by themselves. In other words, sellers are responsible for fulfilling their orders and do not rely on Amazon for the same. If an order for FBM product is placed on your Shopify store, you are responsible for shipping it.&nbsp;
Fulfilling Shopify orders for FBA products

FBA means Fulfilled by Amazon. With it, sellers store their products at Amazon’s fulfillment centers, so that whenever customers place their orders, Amazon picks those products from the fulfillment centers and has them packed, shipped, and delivered at a faster pace. If an order for an FBA product is placed on your Shopify store then you have the following options to get it shipped:
A. Ship it yourself

You can arrange to ship products manually using your preferred shipping partner.
B. Allow Amazon to ship it on your behalf

Use your inventory on Amazon's fulfillment centers to ship it directly to your customers on Shopify. This can be done via the following 2 methods:
1. Use Shopify's 'Fulfillment by Amazon' Service

Shopify has built-in integration with FBA. Once activated, your orders on Shopify will be automatically fulfilled using your inventory on Amazon fulfillment centers. For more information and activating this method follow the instructions provided here.
Note:

This service is only available for Amazon sellers in the United States and Canada.
You will have to configure the 'Inventory Location' in the Shipr dashboard to 'Amazon Marketplace Web'.
The inventory for FBA products on Shopify will be managed by Amazon. There have been multiple cases where the inventory has not been updated correctly. Shopify provides minimal support for queries and troubleshooting inventory-related issues. Shipr app cannot update inventory for such products because it gets overwritten by the Amazon service. We do not recommend this method.
2. Use the Amazon FBA Shipr app

The Shipr app will monitor orders on Shopify and automatically ship them via FBA inventory for all eligible SKUs.

Title: Sku Mapping

The app ships via FBA the Shopify SKUs that have a matching SKU on Amazon FBA. When the SKUs on Shopify and Amazon do not match the app does not ship via FBA automatically.&nbsp;
If you have different SKUs on Shopify and Amazon then you can use the SKU mapping service to link the different SKUs so that the app can ship via FBA even if the SKUs don't match.
Consider, you have an item with SKU B07N7MGX9T on Shopify. The same item has the SKU 2Y-QSSP-V4H0 on Amazon FBA US. To link the 2 SKUs go to Shipr dashboard &gt; Settings &gt; SKU Map page and save the 2 SKUs. Check the image below:
When SKU B07N7MGX9T&nbsp; is ordered on Shopify, the app will attempt to ship 2Y-QSSP-V4H0&nbsp; via Amazon FBA.
You can map different SKUs for each Amazon FBA region.&nbsp;

Title: Auto Sync

On activating auto-sync, the Shipr app continuously syncs the inventory of FBA products from Amazon to Shopify in a cyclic pattern.
In each cycle, all the FBA SKUs will be synced once. The time required to complete a cycle depends on the number of products in the store. As soon as all the products are synced, the cycle is complete and a new cycle starts immediately.
This cyclic syncing of products is executed continuously ensuring that the inventory level on Shopify remaining up-to-date with Amazon.
To activate Auto -sync, go to&nbsp;Shipr dashboard&nbsp;&gt;&nbsp;Settings&nbsp;&gt;&nbsp;Sync&nbsp;&gt; Activate&nbsp;Auto -sync. 
Auto-sync is only available on the Gold Plan ($50 per month).

Title: Fast Sync

Fast-sync helps to sync the inventory of FBA products from Amazon to Shopify.
On activating fast-sync, Shipr will monitor new FBA orders on Amazon and update Shopify product inventory accordingly. The app will monitor Amazon FBA orders and update the Shopify inventory&nbsp;once every 15 minutes.
Only the matching SKUs will be synced. This ensures that the stock levels on Amazon and Shopify are the same, and you don't have to manually update them each time there is an order placed on Amazon.
To activate fast-sync, go to Shipr dashboard&nbsp;&gt; Settings&nbsp;&gt; Sync&nbsp;&gt; Activate Fast-sync.
Fast-sync is available on Pro Plan ($20 per month) and above only.

Title: Ship With Fba

You can use the Shipr app to fulfill orders on Shopify using your Amazon FBA account. This&nbsp;is useful for the sake of&nbsp;inventory efficiency. You can leave your stock with Amazon, all in one place, and fulfill both Amazon and Shopify orders with one dashboard. Additionally, you can track both Amazon and Shopify orders in Seller Central, and your inventory will be neatly stored with one provider i.e. Amazon FBA.&nbsp;
HOW IT WORKS
1] Manual Mode

Go to Shipr app &gt; FBA
The FBA Orders page will list orders from Shopify.&nbsp;
Select the items you want to ship via FBA and click on the 'Ship with FBA' button.
The app will prepare an FBA shipment depending on your FBA settings in the Shipr app.
Confirm the shipment by clicking 'Ship with FBA Now'.
Your order will be sent to and fulfilled by Amazon FBA.&nbsp;
The order status will be available on the app dashboard.
Shipr app will update the Shopify order status along with the tracking information.
2] Auto Mode

Instead of manually confirming each FBA shipment you can configure the app to automatically ship with FBA for all eligible orders on Shopify.

Go to Shipr app &gt; FBA &gt; Settings
Select AUTO Mode and hit Save.
Each new order on Shopify will then be attempted to be shipped via FBA.&nbsp;
You will receive confirmation of success or failure via email.
The order status will be available on the app dashboard.
Shipr will update the Shopify order status along with the tracking information.

Title: Shipping Multi Item Orders In Auto Mode

&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;

The Shipr app is designed to work with multi-item orders on Shopify. You can configure the app to either of the following options:&nbsp;
1. Fulfill partial order when a few invalid SKUs detected

Each line item in the order is attempted to be fulfilled via FBA. In case one or more SKUs are not fulfillable (due to invalid SKU, insufficient inventory, etc) and if this option is selected, then the app will partially fulfill the order by shipping the items having valid SKUs. Invalid SKUs will be skipped.
2. Do not fulfill the order when invalid SKUs detected

Each line item in the order is attempted to be fulfilled via FBA. In case one or more SKUs are not fulfillable (due to invalid SKU, insufficient inventory, etc) and&nbsp;if this option is selected, then the entire order will not be shipped at all. Only if all the line item SKUs of the order are valid, then the order will be shipped via FBA.
3. Do not fulfill the order when multiple SKUs&nbsp;detected

With this option, if an order contains more than one SKU then the entire order will not be shipped. Only if the order contains one unique SKU (irrespective of the quantity ordered) then the order will be attempted to be shipped.
4. Do not fulfill the order when multiple items&nbsp;detected

With this option, if an order contains more than one item then the entire order will not be shipped. If the order contains one unique SKU with more than 1 unit ordered, even then the order not be shipped. Only 1 SKU with 1 quantity ordered will be attempted to be shipped.

Title: Shipr App Fulfilling Orders But Not Updating Shopify Troubleshooting 

If Shipr app successfully ships via FBA but does not update the status of orders on Shopify or does not update the tracking information, then it could be due to the following reasons:
1. Misconfigured Shopify Order Processing settings:
Go to Shopify admin &gt; Settings &gt; Checkout &gt; 'Order processing' section
Set 'After an order has been paid' to 'Do not automatically fulfill any of the order's line items' option.
If 'After an order has been paid' is set to 'Automatically fulfill the order‘s line items' then the Shipr app will not update Shopify order status along with tracking information.
2.&nbsp;Shopify's built-in 'Fulfillment by Amazon' integration is active:
If you use the Shipr app to ship products via FBA then you need to deactivate Shopify's built-in 'Fulfillment by Amazon' integration. This integration prevents the Shipr app to update the Shopify order status correctly. Secondly, keeping it activated might lead to multiple shippings for the same order.
Steps to deactivate 'Fulfillment by Amazon' are provided here.

Title: Does Shipr Handle International Orders 

The app does not perform any action on international orders. It just ignores it and will not attempt to fulfill it again. Amazon does not allow shipping FBA orders to other countries via API automatically.
The Shipr app can auto-fulfill based on location. For example, Orders from the UK will be fulfilled via Amazon UK FBA while orders from Germany will be auto-fulfilled via Amazon DE.
Only the following media products are eligible for international Multi-Channel Fulfillment: books, movies, videos, and DVDs (BMVD products). This restriction is enforced by Amazon and we cannot do anything about it.
In such situations, you will have to manually create a fulfillment order via the Amazon dashboard. This restriction is enforced by Amazon and we cannot do anything about it.

Title: Can I Block Certain Orders From Being Fulfilled By Amazon 

Yes, Shipr offers order blacklisting options, allowing you to exclude specific orders from Amazon fulfillment. This could be based on the Product tags and SKUs.
