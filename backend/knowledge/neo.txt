Neo Blocks is built for:
Merchants who want full control.

You care about how each product is presented. You want to educate customers about each variant, show detailed images, and ensure they find the exact style, size, or model that fits their needs.
Brands that value storytelling.

Your product pages are not just for listing prices and specs. You want to tell a story, highlight features, explain differences between options, and create a rich experience that goes beyond ‘Add to Cart’.
Businesses focused on conversion.

A dynamic, detailed, and personalized product page helps customers make faster decisions. By integrating elements like variant descriptions and galleries, you reduce confusion and increase buyer confidence, leading to higher conversions.

Title: Variant Description Product Block

The Variant Description Block empowers you to display short, variant-specific descriptions directly on your product pages. Whether it’s highlighting size details, color differences, material composition, short description, or special usage notes, this block ensures your shoppers have all the information they need to make confident purchasing decisions.
By providing clear, targeted details for each variant, you:

Enhance product transparency
Reduce customer confusion
Improve conversion rates and shopper trust

&nbsp;
How does it works ?
Setting up Variant Descriptions is simple and seamless. Here’s how to get started:
Step 1: Setup the metafield
1. On your app dashboard homepage, locate the "Variant Description" block.
2. Click on "Setup Metafield" to automatically create a variant-level metafield called neo.variant_description.
Step 2: Add descriptions to each variant&nbsp;
1. Go to your Shopify admin and navigate to the specific variant you wish to edit.

2. Scroll down to Metafields and look for "Neo Variant Description".

3. Enter the text you want displayed on your product page – for example, specific features, color notes, or sizing instructions.
Step 3: Save your changes
Click "Save" to ensure your variant descriptions are updated in Shopify.
Step 4: Customize your product template.&nbsp;
Go to "customize theme settings" &gt; Select the Product template &gt; Add and place the "Neo&nbsp;variant description block" as per your requirement.&nbsp;
Step 5: Apply the styling to match your theme
Within the block settings: Adjust font size, style, and color, custom CSS for advanced styling. Preview changes to ensure it blends seamlessly with your store design.
Step 6: Publish it.

Finally, hit “Save” to publish your updated product pages.

Title: Variant Galleries Product Block

The Variant Gallery Block allows you to assign unique images or videos to each product variant on your store. Instead of generic media for all variants, shoppers see exactly what they are selecting, making their buying decisions easier and faster.
Imagine your customer clicks on “Red” for a t-shirt variant, and they instantly see the red t-shirt images and a video showcasing its fit – that’s the level of clarity and professionalism this feature brings to your product pages.
How does it work?
Setting up Variant Galleries is simple and intuitive:
Step 1: Setup the metafield
1. On your app dashboard homepage, locate the "Variant Galleries" block.

2. Click on "Setup Metafield" to automatically create a variant-level metafield called neo.variant_gallery.
2. Assign medias to each variant
1. Go to your Shopify admin and navigate to the specific variant you wish to edit.

2. Scroll down to Metafields and look for "Neo Variant Galleries".

3. Click “Select files” and upload your variant’s images and videos you want to display on your product page.
Step 3: Save your changes
Click "Save" to ensure your variant Galleries are updated in Shopify.
Step 4: Customize your product template.&nbsp;
Go to "Customize theme settings" &gt; Select the Product template &gt; Add and place the "Neo Variant Gallery block" as per your requirement.&nbsp;
Step 5: Apply the styling to match your theme&nbsp;
Within the block settings: adjust background color, border size, and gallery layout styling. Preview changes to ensure it blends seamlessly with your store design.
Step 6: Publish it.
Finally, hit “Save” to publish your updated product pages.
&nbsp;Please Note:
Supported media formats:

For images: JPEG, PNG, WEBP, SVG, HEIC, and GIF. File size must be under 20 MB.

Title: Variant Inventory Status Block

The Variant Inventory Status Block lets you display real-time stock status messages for each product variant based on predefined inventory thresholds. Instead of showing a generic message, you can automatically label variants as “Low Stock,” “Ready to Ship,” or “Out of Stock” depending on their available quantity.
This helps create urgency, sets the right expectations for customers, and improves the overall shopping experience by showing accurate, variant-specific inventory updates.

How does it work?&nbsp;
Step 1: Add the block in your theme editorGo to Online Store → Customize
Open your Product template
Click “Add block” and select Neo – Variant Inventory Status
Position the block wherever you’d like it to appear on the product page.
Step 2: Configure the block settings

Inside the block settings panel, you can:

Set threshold values to determine when to show:

Low Stock
In Stock
Out of Stock
Customize messaging for each status (e.g., &nbsp;“Out of Stock — check back soon”)
Style the block by adjusting:

Text color, font size
Alignment
Animations or highlight effects
Preview your changes live to ensure the look and position match your store’s design.&nbsp;
Step 3: Save &amp; Publish

Once everything looks good:

Click Save in the theme editor
Your updated product pages will now display variant-specific inventory status automatically based on your configured thresholds.

Title: The Variant Description Block Still Appears Even After Deleting The Metafield What Should I Do 

If the Variant Description block still appears after deleting the "neo.variant_description" metafield, it’s likely because the field’s saved values were not removed. You can fix this in either of the following ways:
Option 1 – Remove the block from your theme

In Shopify Admin, go to Online Store &gt; Themes &gt; Customize.
Open the product page template where the Variant Description block is added.
Locate the block, remove it, and click Save.
Option 2 – Fully delete the metafield

Recreate the Variant Description block in the app — this will restore the "neo.variant_description" metafield.
Delete it again, making sure to choose "Delete field and its saved values" so it’s completely removed from your store.

You may find more info on deleting metafield here.

Title: How To Delete Metafields Created Using The Neo App Block 

The steps to delete metafields created with Neo App blocks are the same for all.
For example, you can follow the instructions provided for the variant description block. 

When you set up the Variant Description Block in the app, a metafield "&nbsp;neo.variant_description " is automatically created at the variant level. If you ever wish to remove this metafield, you have two options depending on whether you want to keep or delete the existing content on your product pages.
Follow the steps below to delete the metafield:
Step 1: Access the Variant Metafields

From your Shopify Admin, go to Settings&nbsp;
Under Metafields and Metaobjects, select Variants.
You’ll now see a list of metafields associated with your product variants.
Step 2: Delete the Metafield&nbsp;

Locate the Neo Variant Description metafield (usually named neo.variant_description).
Click on the three dots (•••) next to the field and choose Delete definition.
Shopify will show two delete options:

Delete the field onlyThis removes the metafield definition, but keeps existing values. The block will still display on product pages if values were added.
Delete the field and its saved valuesThis removes the metafield definition and all stored values. The block will no longer appear on product pages.

Title: Can I Use My Own Metafield To Show Content In The Variant Description Block 

Yes, you can use your own existing metafield ( variant level ) &nbsp;with the Variant Description Block in the Neo app.
To do this:

Add the Variant Description Block to your product template via Online Store &gt; Customize in your Shopify admin.
In the block settings, enter the Namespace and Key of your existing variant-level metafield.
If the metafield exists for a variant, the block will automatically display the content
Note: The block will only appear if the metafield is present and contains a value for the selected variant.

This gives you full control to manage content using your existing metafields without needing to recreate them in the app.
