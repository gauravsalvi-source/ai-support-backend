Title: Spreadr App With Amazon Affiliate Program How It Works 

BigCommerce store owners can use Spreadr to make extra money via Amazon Affiliate Program.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

How it works:

&nbsp;

1.&nbsp;&nbsp;Install&nbsp;Spreadr App.

2. Import complementary products from Amazon to your store via Spreadr App.

3. Each imported product will have a View on Amazon button.&nbsp;

4. Clicking View on Amazon button will redirect the users to Amazon where they buy the product. Amazon fulfills the order.

5. You get paid commission for all the sales generated via your referred customer.&nbsp;

6. Important - you need to sign up for Amazon Affiliate Program and save the Affiliate ID in the settings section of Spreadr dashboard.

7. Amazon will transfer the payment to your account as per the settings in your Amazon Affiliate Program Account.
&nbsp;

Most of the Spreadr App users use this option to generate additional revenue to complement their existing source.

&nbsp;

* If a visitor to your site buys something from Amazon within 24 hours of clicking on your affiliate link, you will get a commission. You get commissions for every single item they buy - not just the ones you promote and link out to. i.e you will make money every time someone clicks on one of your links and purchases anything from Amazon.

Title: Spreadr App For Dropshipping How It Works 

Sellers who use Amazon as their main dropshipping source can use Spreadr to effortlessly import products to their BigCommerce store.&nbsp;
How it works?
1. Install&nbsp;Spreadr App.

2. Import products from Amazon to your store via Spreadr App.

3. Markup the product price via BigCommerce admin.

4. Here you do not linkout (redirect) your customers to Amazon.

5. Customers place the order on your site and you have to fulfill it.

6. It is like selling a regular product on your site - you receive the payment from your users.&nbsp;

7. Amazon is not the drop-shipper. You are responsible for sourcing the products and delivering to your customers. You have to provide customer service.
This option is best suited for people who sell products on Amazon to also have them on their BigCommerce website.&nbsp;
We do not recommend drop-shipping for those who are looking to place manual order on Amazon in response to their customers placing an order on BigCommerce site. It leads to fulfillment, customer service and IP issues.

Title: Product Types

Products imported via Spreadr app can be of three types:&nbsp;
1. Affiliate Downloadable Products

These products will have a 'View on Amazon' button. When users click this button they will be redirected to Amazon in a new browser tab. The benefit of adding affiliate products is that Amazon takes care of inventory, tax and shipping. You earn commissions on all the sales you refer to Amazon. These products will be added as 'Downloadable product' (not 'Physical product') in BigCommerce. This is to prevent these products from being published on Facebook and Instagram sales channels.

&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;

2. Drop-shipping Physical Products

These are the regular 'Physical' products on BigCommerce with an 'Add to Cart' button.
3. &nbsp;Affiliate Physical Products
These products will have a 'View on Amazon' button. When users click this button they will be redirected to Amazon in a new browser tab. These products will be added as 'Physical' products in BigCommerce. They can published on Facebook and Instagram sales channels.
Note:

'Quick View' button will be hidden for affiliate products on category pages. The Quick View popup contains a dynamically generated 'Add to Cart' button which allows the product to be checked out on BigCommerce.

Title: Product Variants

Spreadr app does not import product variants/options (sizes, colors etc).&nbsp;&nbsp;
Most of the leading affiliate stores do not display variants. They only show primary product and leave the variants, shipping details etc to the destination site. Examples - canopy.com, thisiswhyimbroke.com, thegatdgetflow.com, lyst.com.&nbsp;
This is done primarily to get the affiliate 'click' and redirect the user to the destination site. Once the visitor is on destination site (Amazon in our case) you get commissions for any variant they buy - not just the ones you promote and link out to. Not only that, you make money if users purchase anything else from Amazon.
Moreover, importing variants significantly increases the time required to import products and introduces complexities in auto-syncing.

Title: Auto Tagging 

Auto-tagging allows the stores to automatically add BigCommerce product tags while importing products from Amazon. Tags are added based on the product information derived from Amazon.&nbsp;

&nbsp;

Users can select specific tags which need to be auto-added while importing the products.

&nbsp;

Example - if the users select Brand and ProductGroup as auto-tags while importing a Nike Men's Tanjun Premium Shoe then two product tags - 1. Nike and 2. Shoes - will be auto added to the the product while importing it from Amazon to BigCommerce.

&nbsp;

Note:

Auto-tagging cannot be applied retrospectively to the products already imported before.
If the tag information derived from Amazon is empty then the tag will not be added.

Title: What Is Auto Sync 

The Auto-Sync feature ensures your BigCommerce store always reflects the latest Amazon product prices and availability, keeping your inventory up to date.

Spreadr auto-syncs each product once every 24 hours.
Available for Pro plan and above.
Once activated, auto-sync applies to all products in the Spreadr dashboard.

Auto-Sync Data OptionsSpreadr offers two auto-sync methods to update products:

✅ Auto-Sync with AWS API (Recommended)Uses Amazon API for product updates.
Requires Amazon Web Services (AWS) Access Credentials setup. [More info here.]
Provides higher product limits and more reliable data.
Fetches the Buy Box price of the product.

✅ Auto-Sync without AWS APIUses alternative sources of Amazon data (no AWS credentials required).
Supports lower product limits (see the plans page for details).
Fetches the lowest offer price of the product.

Important Notes:⚠️ Variants manually added after importing a product will be deleted during auto-sync.⚠️ Auto-sync with AWS API fetches the Buy Box price, while auto-sync without AWS API fetches the lowest offer price.

Title: What Is Markup Pricing 

Markup Pricing&nbsp;allows you to&nbsp;add a margin or markup&nbsp;to the products you import from Amazon. Spreadr will either&nbsp;multiply&nbsp;or&nbsp;add a fixed amount&nbsp;to the original Amazon price based on the rules you define in the&nbsp;Markup Settings&nbsp;page.&nbsp;&nbsp;

How Markup Pricing WorksApplies to all new products imported via Spreadr.
If Auto-Sync is enabled, the marked-up prices will be updated automatically based on your Markup Pricing rules.
Existing products (imported before enabling Markup Pricing) will not be affected unless auto-sync updates them.

⚠️ Important: Markup Pricing is only meant for dropshipping products. If your store is an Amazon Affiliate store only, you should not use this feature, as Amazon requires affiliate stores to display real-time prices.
For BigCommerce&nbsp;Navigate to Dashboard &gt; Settings &gt; Markup Pricing
Set your markup rules (percentage or fixed value)
Click Save

✅ Your pricing rules are now set, and products will be imported with the defined markup!

Title: What Is Geo Localize 

Amazon eCommerce is not a single globalized store, rather is a collection of country and region-specific storefronts. Each of those storefronts has its own separate affiliate programs as well. This means your Amazon.com associates ID is not earning you commissions&nbsp;in any country but the US.&nbsp;
Spreadr solves this problem to an extent via the Geo-localize feature. It detects the location of your users and takes them to their country-specific Amazon storefronts (eg: a UK visitor to amazon.co.uk and a US visitor to amazon.com). It allows you to add every country's affiliate ID into the dashboard, then automatically appends the country/region-specific affiliate ID for each link before sending the user on to their local storefront.&nbsp;
How it works?
1️⃣ The first step we take is to detect the location (country) of the user.&nbsp;
2️⃣ If the user is coming from the same country as your Amazon link, no localization is needed. The user is simply sent to the original link. For example, if the link was built using the US Amazon storefront, amazon.com, a user from the US would simply be directed to the original product listing in the Amazon US storefront.
3️⃣ If the user is coming from another country, we check if the country has a native Amazon storefront:

• If a native Amazon storefront is found, we do a generic search within the destination storefront using the product title and guide the user to the search result page with the affiliate ID embedded in it.
• If a native Amazon storefront is not found, we send the user to the original product link. We will soon be introducing country grouping, wherein countries with no Amazon storefront will be redirected to the nearest / most relevant Amazon storefront. For example, a user from Austria will be redirected to Amazon Germany.
This allows you not only to ensure a better user experience but also to receive all of your international affiliate commissions. &nbsp;
Geo-localize is only available for pro plan&nbsp;account users and above.
Note: This feature is provided on a best effort basis – while most of the time, this service should be able to detect the visitor country and redirect them to the right local store, it may not work all the time. We expect the accuracy to be more than 97%, but there is a small chance that the visitor redirect may not happen as expected.
